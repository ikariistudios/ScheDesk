What you get

  • Weekly grid and daily agendaSee the whole week, or just what is next today.
  • Import by scanningBring in a schedule from a photo or document instead of typing it.
  • Class remindersGet a notification before each class starts.
  • Instructors and groupsKeep teachers and class groups organized.
  • Export to ExcelSave your schedule as a spreadsheet.
  • Your lookPick an accent color and add stickers.

How to install

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. Tap Install, then open ScheDesk.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
